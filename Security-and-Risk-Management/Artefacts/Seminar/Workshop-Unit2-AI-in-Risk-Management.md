# Unit 2 Seminar Workshop – The Role of AI in Risk Management

## Context
This workshop is based on: Kalogiannidis, S., Kalfas, D., Papaevangelou, O., Giannarakis, G. and Chatzitheodoridis, F. (2024) ‘The role of artificial intelligence technology in predictive risk assessment for business continuity: a case study of Greece’, *Risks*, 12(2), p. 19.

## Questions and answers

### 1) How does NLP improve the efficiency and accuracy of risk assessment processes in businesses?
This paper shows that NLP can improve risk assessment by making the process faster and more accurate. Kalogiannidis et al. (2024) explain that NLP helps organisations analyse large amounts of unstructured data, 
such as reports, emails, and other textual information, which would take much longer to review manually. This is important because many early signs of risk appear in text rather than in formal metrics. As a result, NLP can support quicker identification of threats and better-informed decision-making.

### 2) In what ways can AI-powered data analytics enhance risk prediction and support business continuity in a dynamic corporate environment?
The article also shows that AI-powered data analytics can strengthen risk prediction and support business continuity in a dynamic business environment. 
By processing large volumes of data and identifying patterns, AI can help organisations anticipate disruptions, respond more rapidly, and improve resilience. In other words, AI does not only help businesses react to risk, but also supports a more proactive approach by forecasting possible problems before they escalate.

### 3) Why is it important for businesses to integrate multiple AI technologies, beyond just NLP, into their risk management strategies?
However, the paper makes clear that NLP alone is not enough. Businesses should integrate multiple AI technologies into their risk management strategies because risks are complex and come from different sources. 
While NLP is useful for analysing textual data, other AI tools, such as predictive analytics and automated monitoring systems, are needed to capture operational, financial, and supply chain risks more effectively. Therefore, a combination of AI technologies provides a broader and more reliable foundation for business continuity and long-term resilience.
